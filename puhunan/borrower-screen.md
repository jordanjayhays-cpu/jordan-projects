# Borrower screen — NEW-business version (proven skill, staged, with skin in the game)

We fund BRAND-NEW businesses — but only where the RISK is managed. New business + proven skill is
fundable; new business + unproven dream is a coin flip. This screen is the whole game.

## The 7 questions (fund only when most are green)

1. **Proven skill or existing demand?** ← THE key filter. Can they ALREADY do the thing, and do
   people already want it? (The cook everyone asks to cater · the seamstress with a waitlist · the
   driver who needs his own tricycle.) The *business* is new; the *skill/demand* is not.
   - Green: proven skill + people already ask for it.  Red: "I've never done it but I want to try."
2. **Do I / my family know them, or does someone trusted vouch?** Still the #1 repayment predictor.
3. **Cheap to start + obvious local demand?** Low startup cost + a clear market beats big + speculative.
4. **Skin in the game?** Will they put in a little of their own money or unpaid labor first? People
   fight for what they co-funded. No skin = higher risk.
5. **Willing to be featured?** They must agree to be filmed/photographed for their Puhunan social
   story — the marketing IS part of the deal (that's how we bring them customers). Camera-shy = not a fit.
6. **What's my downside?** Only fund what you can fully lose without pain. Assume ~half won't repay.
7. **Milestone for staged money?** Start SMALL (e.g. ₱10k). Define one milestone (first month of
   sales, first 20 customers) before releasing the rest. Never hand ₱30k to an unproven idea day one.

## Portfolio mindset (this is not a loan book)
Expect ~half to fail. The winners' profit-share must cover the losers. So price for upside on
successes, stage the money, and let the marketing layer raise everyone's odds. You are a micro-
INCUBATOR, not a lender.
