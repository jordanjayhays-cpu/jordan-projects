# Borrower screen — 5 questions before funding anyone

Fund only when most answers are strong. This screen IS the business — bad picks kill the model.

1. **Can they already sell?** Have they run *any* small business or sold anything before? (Existing
   sari-sari owners wanting to restock = lowest risk. First-timers with zero track record = highest.)
2. **Do I / my family actually know them, or know someone who vouches?** No personal link = don't fund
   (yet). This is the #1 predictor of repayment.
3. **Is the plan concrete and cheap to start?** "₱30k for inventory for my existing stall" beats
   "₱30k for my new idea." Concrete + already-proven demand > exciting + unproven.
4. **What's my downside if it's a total loss?** Only fund what you can fully lose without pain. At
   $500/deal, cap total exposure to what you'd shrug off.
5. **Do they have daily/weekly cash flow to repay from?** Food carts, stores, services with daily
   sales = yes. One-off projects with no recurring income = hard to repay.

Green = mostly yes. Fund. | Yellow = mixed. Smaller amount or wait. | Red = no personal link, no
track record, no recurring cash flow. Pass, kindly.
