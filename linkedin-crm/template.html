Add new connections

Two ways, both instant and entirely in this browser — no export, no waiting.
Quickest: open your LinkedIn connections page, scroll until you reach people you already have, select all (⌘A / Ctrl+A), copy, and paste below.
Most complete (adds profile links, companies and job titles): drop in the Connections.csv from a LinkedIn data export.

Add connections whenever you like — paste the LinkedIn page or drop a Connections.csv, and they merge in instantly, right here in the browser. Everything you add, plus your stages, stars and notes, is saved in this browser only. Use Backup to download all of it as one file and Restore to load it on another device. Export CSV gives you the full list with your pipeline data — ready for Sheets, HubSpot or Notion.
