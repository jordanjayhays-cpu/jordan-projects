## The science backs one paying anchor lane, finished in bounded units

The peer-reviewed literature does not give a formula for splitting a solo founder's week across four ventures, and the report says so plainly before using it. What it does give is three results that point the same way. First, keeping a paying activity while testing ventures beats going all-in: Raffiee and Feng's 2014 study of hybrid entrepreneurs found that those who entered self-employment while keeping wage work and only later went full-time had "much higher rates of survival" than those who jumped directly, with the advantage attributed to learning **[PR]** ([Raffiee & Feng 2014, AMJ](https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/amj.2012.0522)); Folta, Delmar and Wennberg model the same hybrid entry as a low-cost way to learn before committing **[PR]** ([Folta et al. 2010, Management Science](https://ideas.repec.org/a/inm/ormnsc/v56y2010i2p253-269.html)). The often-repeated "33% lower exit hazard" magnitude is **UNSURE**; the abstract supports only the direction. Second, switching is expensive in exactly Jordan's situation: Rubinstein, Meyer and Evans showed that switch costs rise with rule complexity and fall with familiarity **[PR]** ([Rubinstein et al. 2001](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11518143/)), and Leroy found that unfinished tasks leave "attention residue" that degrades the next task, while finishing a bounded unit under time pressure before switching removes most of the penalty **[PR]** ([Leroy 2009, OBHDP](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0749597809000399)). The popular "multitasking costs 40% of productive time" is not a published result; the APA attributes it to a remark by David Meyer, so it is **UNSURE** ([APA](https://www.apa.org/topics/research/multitasking)). Four domains with different rules (recruitment compliance, a consumer booking product, agency contracts, a content channel) are the high-cost case, which is why the operating rule below is "finish the list, then switch", not "never switch".

Third, the only randomised evidence on how founders should run early ventures says the winning behaviour is to state a revenue hypothesis, test it cheaply with a paying or committing customer, and terminate on a negative result. Camuffo and colleagues' 2020 RCT on 116 Italian startups found founders taught this discipline performed better and pivoted more without dropping out **[PR, RCT]** ([Camuffo et al. 2020, Management Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2018.3249)); the 2024 replication across **759 firms** in four RCTs raised the probability of terminating an idea by **9.8 percentage points** and concentrated pivots at one or two, a pattern that correlated with higher performance **[PR, RCT]** ([Camuffo et al. 2024, SMJ](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/smj.3580)). The failure base rates agree on the cause to test for: of 431 failed VC-backed startups, **43%** had poor product-market fit as the root cause **[PRAC]** ([CB Insights, 2024](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/startup-failure-reasons-top/)). Massage Club, a working product with zero paying users after 41 bookings, is precisely the false positive this protocol exists to catch; that is not a verdict on the product, it is the reason the lane gets a dated pre-sell test rather than more build time (inference).

Two further results shape the plan's texture. Hamilton's classic study found median self-employment earnings **35% below** the predicted alternative wage after ten years **[PR]** ([Hamilton 2000, JPE](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/262131)), and the Federal Reserve's 2025 survey found **59%** of self-employed adults report month-to-month income variation versus **28%** of employees **[LD]** ([SHED, May 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2025-economic-well-being-of-us-households-in-2024-income-and-expenses.htm)); JPMorgan Chase Institute data on 240,000 platform earners show labour-platform participants earned in only **41%** of subsequent months and over half exited within a year, while those who persisted relied on that income for 47% of active-month earnings **[LD]** ([JPMC Institute, 2016](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/all-topics/labor-markets-and-workforce/jpmc-institute-online-platform-econ-brief)). Together these say commission income stabilises with continuity, not with dipping in and out, which argues for daily presence in Placewell rather than bursts. Finally, Masicampo and Baumeister showed that unfulfilled goals intrude on unrelated work, and that writing a specific plan for each goal eliminated the interference even when the task stayed undone **[PR]** ([Masicampo & Baumeister 2011, JPSP](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21688924/)); the Zeigarnik memory effect itself fails to replicate (recall ratio **0.99** across 38 publications) though the urge to resume unfinished tasks holds **[PR, meta-analysis]** ([Nature HSSC, 2025](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-025-05000-w)), and ego-depletion, the basis for "decide early in the day", came out at **d = 0.04** in a 23-lab registered replication **[PR]** ([Hagger et al. 2016](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/abs/10.1177/1745691616652873)). The practical consequence for a founder carrying roughly 35 open tasks is cheap: every task gets a dated next step or a written "parked until" note, and no plan should rely on decision fatigue. Where the evidence is weakest: no study measures income loss as a function of number of ventures, portfolio-entrepreneur studies such as Westhead and Wright concern owners of several operating businesses with teams **[PR]** ([Westhead & Wright 2003](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/regstd/v37y2003i2p187-200.html)), and the review cadence below (weekly metrics, monthly gate) is a defensible default taken from the RCTs' 3–4-week measurement rhythm, not an optimum.

### Why zero email replies is expected, and what converts instead

Jordan's zero replies from cold email to European employers is not a signal about Placewell; it is what the largest 2025 dataset predicts. Belkins sent **7,530,489** cold emails for clients in 2025 and recorded a **0.45%** reply rate (unique replies over total sends, auto-replies excluded), booking "over 1,200 appointments", roughly one per 6,000 emails **[PRAC, large dataset]** ([Belkins, updated Jun 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates)). Two hundred emails yield about one reply. Phone does better per contact, especially in Europe: across 175,000+ dials Belkins found about one meeting per **370 dials**, a UK positive-outcome rate of **2.84%** per dial against 0.55% in the US, non-US markets at 1.6–7x US rates, best hours at 3–4 PM and 6 PM local, best meeting months February (8.9%) and November (6.5%) with a July–September trough, and directors producing the most booked meetings while C-level positives were all follow-up requests **[PRAC]** ([Belkins calling benchmarks, 2026](https://belkins.io/blog/cold-calling-benchmarks)). Gong's 300-million-call corpus shows email reply rates roughly doubling, **1.81% to 3.44%**, when a call accompanies the email **[PRAC]**, year **UNSURE** ([Gong](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-hidden-power-of-cold-calling-insights-from-300m-calls)). The weak point is that none of this is staffing-specific; Belkins reports no HR vertical and no Japanese cooperative data, so treat the generic funnel as a floor for kumiai, who are the buyer side of a legally mandated channel (inference). The strongest timing argument is also the cheapest: the only large study of lead response, an audit of 2,241 US companies, found firms contacting a lead within an hour were nearly **7x** more likely to qualify it than those waiting an hour longer and more than 60x more likely than those waiting 24 hours, and 19% of Belkins' calls ended in a requested call-back; same-day call-back discipline on every warm signal is the conversion lever with the best evidence and no cost **[PRAC, OLD 2011]** ([HBR, Mar 2011](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)). One time-zone inference matters for the rhythm: Belkins' best window of 3–4 PM Tokyo time is 7–8 AM in Madrid, so the kumiai block belongs at 07:00–09:30 CET, and the Europe block at 09:00–11:00 CET.

## Massage Club: fix the five-minute reply, then sell December vouchers

Massage Club's 13 cold requests are consistent with response time, not demand, being the bottleneck. ### Spanish agency law, LinkedIn terms and LSSI

Three legal facts should be settled before the first campaign, and none of this is legal advice. First, if Amigo negotiates sales on a principal's behalf continuously for commission, it is a commercial agent under Ley 12/1992, Spain's transposition of Directive 86/653, and gains a non-waivable goodwill indemnity on termination capped at one year's average remuneration (art. 28), cumulative damages for unamortised costs (art. 29), and a one-year limitation period (art. 31); Directive art. 19 forbids derogation to the agent's detriment, and the CJEU's Ingmar ruling (C-381/98, 2000) applies these rights where the agent works in a Member State even if a non-EU principal chooses its own law, which is a genuine negotiating asset with Mexican, Indian or Turkish principals ([BOE, Ley 12/1992](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1992-12347); [Directive 86/653](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/1986/653/oj/eng); [Rödl & Partner, 2025](https://www.roedl.es/es/articulos/blog2025/indemnizacion-clientela-contrato-agencia); [Ingmar, BAILII](https://www.bailii.org/eu/cases/EUECJ/2000/C38198.html)). If Amigo only generates and hands over leads, the law probably does not apply; the structure should be chosen deliberately with counsel, a Jordan task. Second, running HeyReach from a named representative's account breaches LinkedIn's User Agreement on account sharing (s.2.2, s.8.2(1)) and unauthorised automation (s.8.2(13)), and the account owner bears the restriction risk; the "23% restricted within 90 days" figure circulating in 2026 has no traceable methodology and is **UNSURE** ([LinkedIn User Agreement, fetched 24 Nov 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement); [ConnectSafely, 2026](https://connectsafely.ai/articles/does-linkedin-allow-automation-policy-guide-2026)). The structure that reduces but does not remove the risk is for the representative to keep sole credential control and run the tool themselves under a written mandate, with a truthful profile role, a contractual 100–150 requests-per-week cap and an explicit allocation of restriction risk; manual sending through Sales Navigator is the only fully compliant route (inference). Third, Spain's LSSI art. 21 bans unsolicited commercial email to natural and legal persons alike absent a prior relationship, the AEPD has held it overrides GDPR legitimate interest for the act of sending, and serious breaches carry fines of **€30,001–150,000** ([Imisofts, 2026](https://imisofts.com/blog/cold-email-laws-spain/); [Rafa Gandia](https://rafagandia.com/blog/cold-email-rgpd-espana/)). The practitioner reading that role-based company addresses with opt-out and a documented legitimate-interest assessment are workable, and that LinkedIn invitations fall outside art. 21, is asserted without AEPD authority and is **UNSURE** ([Overloop ES](https://overloop.com/es/blog/b2b-cold-email-espana-rgpd-aepd)). The practical conclusion is to build Amigo's engine on LinkedIn and warm introductions, keep email secondary and role-based, and prefer target markets such as the UK, Netherlands and Nordics with explicit B2B opt-out regimes, where reply rates are also higher (Netherlands 10.5% LinkedIn reply, Poland 1.43% email) (inference from the Belkins country data).

## A 60/25/15 rule, a December exception and a four-block week

The allocation rule is a twelve-week hypothesis, not a measured optimum, and the research notes say so: no study gives an optimal split for this profile, and scaling SDR medians to a part-time student seller is a linear assumption the Bridge Group data do not test. With that caveat, the evidence supports roughly **60%** of available work hours to Placewell, because hybrid-entry survival, Hamilton's earnings gap and the volatility data on persistence all favour continuous presence in the one lane with a paying buyer; **25%** to Amigo, pitched as a paid pilot and run in the same calling blocks and CRM so that the switch cost is low, with the honest caveat that the buyers differ (kumiai and FPAs versus SME exporters) and the shared element is the selling motion rather than the persona; **15%** to Massage Club as a pre-sell test with a dated kill criterion; and **0%** to Niah and Philosophical King beyond a one-page plan each, which the plan-making evidence says removes their intrusion cost. The December exception is an inference from two timing facts: gift-card sales concentrate 15–24 December, and Belkins' calling data show Japanese and European connect rates will sag through year-end holidays the way its July–September trough does, so from 1 to 24 December Massage Club rises to about 30% and Placewell falls to about 50%, reverting on 2 January when Belkins' peak meeting months (February 8.9%) and Japan's April-start hiring make outbound most productive.

The weekly rhythm turns the benchmarks into blocks. A full-time SDR sustains a median **112 touches** a day for 4.1 quality conversations and a quota of **10 held meetings** a month, with only 60% hitting it **[PRAC, survey n=351]** ([Bridge Group, Feb 2025](https://www.bridgegroupinc.com/research/2025-sdr-models-metrics-report-the-bridge-group)); reps spend only 30% of their week selling ([Salesforce State of Sales, 2024](https://assets.ctfassets.net/f43wltp2j5se/2gHMpCURXzpMW7PJ3SlWZJ/3cad8d7e8496abbd3c0f99d5c7f16ef4/salesforce-state-of-sales-report-6-ed.pdf)); Gong's connect rate averages 5.4% ([Gong](https://www.gong.io/blog/the-hidden-power-of-cold-calling-insights-from-300m-calls)). A part-time seller with a third of that selling time should expect 25–40 touches and 1–2 real conversations a day and **8–10 held meetings a month** across Placewell and Amigo combined (inference). The famous "80% of sales need five follow-ups" has no study behind it, tracing to a 1942 survey of under 40 respondents and a 2009 column ([AInora source trace, 2026](https://ainora.lt/blog/sales-follow-up-80-percent-5-contacts-study-2026)); the only large dataset, 12 million outreach emails, found one follow-up associated with **65.8%** more replies and contacting several people at one firm with 93% higher response, in a PR context **[PRAC]** ([Backlinko, 2019](https://backlinko.com/email-outreach-study)), so plan three touches and two contacts per target, not five.

| Block | When (Madrid time) | What, and why | Finish-before-switch unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Japan calls | Mon–Thu 07:00–09:30 (15:00–17:30 Tokyo, Belkins' best hours) | Placewell kumiai list, 2–3 calls plus same-day email; segment by open-cap sector and DMW accreditation | The day's call slice, logged with outcome |
| Europe outbound | Mon–Thu 09:30–11:00 | Placewell FPAs, Czech sponsors, Italian associations; Amigo warm-contact calls in the same block; no product work | One list per day |
| Inbound SLA | All day | Massage Club WhatsApp requests answered with two slots and a Bizum link inside 5 minutes (15 out of hours); any Placewell or Amigo warm signal called back the same day | P90 response time, never-answered rate |
| Build and admin | Mon–Thu afternoon, one lane per day | Massage Club voucher page and studio agreements (Dec); Amigo pilot offer and representative mandate; Placewell milestone-billing template | One deliverable closed per session (Leroy) |
| Weekly review | Friday, 60 minutes | Leading metrics per lane: touches, conversations, meetings held, proposals out, referrals asked and received, paid bookings/requests, EUR invoiced; plan-making pass over every open task (next step or park date) | The task list reaches zero undated items |
| Monthly gate | Last Friday of each month | Reverse income statement per lane; apply each kill rule; one pivot at most | Written continue/kill/pivot decision per lane |

The monthly gate borrows discovery-driven planning, which starts from required profit, lists the assumptions and releases resources only when the previous milestone's evidence justifies the next step **[PRAC, HBR]** ([McGrath & MacMillan, 1995](https://hbr.org/1995/07/discovery-driven-planning)), and real-options reasoning, which treats each lane as a staged option to be exercised or abandoned cheaply **[PR, theory]** ([McGrath 1999, AMR](https://journals.aom.org/doi/abs/10.5465/AMR.1999.1580438)). Experiment line for the whole system: hypothesis, concentrating about 85% of selling time on Placewell and Amigo in fixed morning blocks produces more held meetings and more signed revenue than the current spread; metrics, held meetings a month, proposals a month, placement fees and pilot fees invoiced, Massage Club paid commitments, hours logged per lane; time frame, twelve weeks from 1 December 2026, reviewed weekly and gated monthly; sample, all outbound in the period, expected at 1,200–2,000 touches.

## Open questions that would most change the conclusion

The first and largest is Placewell's own economics: what the agency earns per placement by destination, when the cash arrives relative to signing and arrival, what Jordan's commission percentage and trigger are, and what share of Placewell's 2025 revenue came from the five suspended Gulf states. No public source gives any of these, and the entire ranking rests on Placewell's ticket being large; if Jordan's share of a 10-worker EU cohort is a few hundred euros paid six months after arrival, Amigo moves to first and the 60% drops. Second, the current status of DMW Advisory No. 10 and of Kuwait household-worker processing, both **UNSURE**; a lifted suspension would reopen a 52% market and change the destination mix overnight. Third, what kumiai actually pay a Philippine sending organisation per trainee and whether any of the 87 already have a sending partner or DMW accreditation; the first fact sets Japan's unit economics, the second the speed of first deployment. Fourth, Jordan's real weekly hours around the MBA, which turns the percentages into absolute blocks and determines whether 8–10 held meetings a month is attainable. Fifth, whether the three Madrid studios accept deposit-netting and whether Fresha really waives its 20% new-client fee in Spain; a "yes" to both would make Massage Club's fee both easy to collect and harder to defend against a free incumbent. Sixth, two legal readings for Amigo: whether its planned activity makes it an agent under Ley 12/1992 and whether LinkedIn direct messages count as commercial communications under LSSI art. 21, both unresolved in the sources and requiring Spanish counsel. Seventh, whether the Philippines is among Italy's 38 priority countries for the 2027 cycle and the exact 2027 click-day calendar, which decide whether Italy belongs in the 90-day plan at all. Beyond these, the evidence base has structural holes the reader should hold in mind: every sales-conversion number is vendor data on general B2B, mostly software; every no-show and gift-card figure is UK or US; and no peer-reviewed study tests pre-selling, retainers versus per-placement fees, or review cadence.

## Conclusion

The research reframes Jordan's own ordering. He listed Massage Club first because it works, but the hypothesis-testing RCTs say a working product with no payers is the state most in need of a cheap, dated test, not more attention, and the incumbents' convergence on first-booking-only fees means its ceiling is tens of euros per client unless a corporate or recurring buyer appears. He placed Placewell second because it is someone else's company, but the demand data say it is the only lane where a published, growing buyer universe (3,755 kumiai under a 1.23-million cap) meets a legally mandated intermediary role and a ticket in the thousands, and the Gulf suspension makes redirecting that pipeline urgent rather than optional. Amigo's value is real but conditional: a paid pilot sold warm is the only structure the evidence supports, and the legal frame, agency law protecting Jordan, LinkedIn terms exposing the representative, and LSSI constraining email, should be set before the first message rather than after the first complaint.

What the 6–12-month picture implies is a sequencing, not a portfolio. December belongs to Massage Club's voucher window and to building the kumiai call list; January and February, Belkins' peak meeting season and Japan's pre-April hiring run, belong to phone-first outbound for Placewell with Amigo's pilot sold alongside; by the end of February each lane will have either a paying customer or a written kill decision, which the Camuffo results say is the outcome to want. If Placewell's own numbers, once extracted, show that its cash per placement for Jordan is small or slow, the same rule applies to it, and Amigo's retainer becomes the anchor; the method is more durable than any single allocation.

## Three objections Jordan will raise, and the answers

"Massage Club is the one thing that actually works, and you want me to cut it to 15%." The 41 bookings prove the matching works; they do not prove anyone will pay for it, and 43% of post-mortemed startup failures trace to exactly that gap ([CB Insights, 2024](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/startup-failure-reasons-top/)). Fifteen percent, lifted to 30% for the December voucher window, is enough to run the only two tests that matter, the five-minute reply with a deposit and a voucher or corporate sale, and the RCT evidence says a founder who runs that test and acts on the result does better than one who keeps building ([Camuffo et al. 2024](https://sms.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/smj.3580)). If 20 requests convert at 20% or better, the allocation rises at the January gate.

"Placewell commission is volatile and half its market just closed; spreading my bets is the safer play." The volatility data say commission income stabilises for those who stay in continuously, not for those who dip in and out ([JPMC Institute, 2016](https://www.jpmorganchase.com/institute/all-topics/labor-markets-and-workforce/jpmc-institute-online-platform-econ-brief)), and the portfolio-entrepreneur literature that seems to support spreading concerns owners of several operating businesses with teams, not one person with pre-revenue ideas ([Westhead & Wright 2003](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/regstd/v37y2003i2p187-200.html)); the switching and attention-residue costs are measured, the diversification benefit for this profile is not ([Leroy 2009](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0749597809000399)). The hedge against the Gulf is not another lane, it is Japan and central Europe inside the same lane, plus Amigo's retainer as the monthly base.

"Everyone runs LinkedIn automation and cold email; the legal caution is overkill for a one-person shop." The terms and the law are not ambiguous: LinkedIn bans account sharing and unauthorised automation in its current User Agreement and the restriction falls on the representative, not Amigo ([LinkedIn User Agreement](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/user-agreement)), and LSSI art. 21 fines of €30,001–150,000 apply to B2B email from Spain, with a 2024 SME fined for a broken unsubscribe link alone ([Imisofts, 2026](https://imisofts.com/blog/cold-email-laws-spain/); [Overloop ES](https://overloop.com/es/blog/b2b-cold-email-espana-rgpd-aepd)). The structure recommended, representative-held credentials under a written mandate, a 100–150-a-week cap, role-based email with opt-out, costs nothing in volume that the benchmarks say Amigo could use anyway, since one profile yields 1–2 meetings a month at any compliant pace.
