Design system

Philosophy set to music. 250+ tracks, daily short-form video, an audience that is roughly 90% Philippines.

Colour — monochrome teal and cyan, nothing else
#0e0f13 ground
#c8e0d8 teal
#e9c46a gold
#b09a4a gold dim
#7a7e87 grey

There is no second accent colour. Emphasis comes from gold or from scale.

Type — serif throughout

Memento mori

The cave fell behind

Break the chains, step outside

Serif reads as inscription rather than interface. Gold takes one landing word, never a whole line. Shadows are hard, never blurred. Nothing rounds.

Video safe zones — 1080×1920 native canvas

Short-form platform UI eats the top and bottom of the frame. These are measured positions from the shipping renderer, not guesses.

  • y = 200 — watermark, below the top UI band
  • y ≈ 1380 — lyric line, above the bottom UI band
  • 1100–1760 — needs a scrim behind it, or teal text vanishes against teal imagery

Never open on black. Measured: a video that faded in from black retained 48% where the same-length alternatives retained 58–75%.
