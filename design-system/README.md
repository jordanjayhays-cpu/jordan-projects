# Philosophical King — design system

Philosophy set to music. 250+ tracks, daily short-form video, an audience that is
roughly 90% Philippines.

- `tokens.css` — colour, type, form and video safe-zone tokens
- `components.css` — wordmark, type scale, gold rule, cards, buttons, quote card,
  social post, scrim
- `index.html` — a rendered showcase of every component

## The rules that matter

**Monochrome teal and cyan, nothing else.** There is no second accent colour.
Emphasis comes from gold or from scale.

**Serif throughout.** It reads as inscription rather than interface, which is the
point of the project.

**Nothing rounds.** `border-radius: 0` everywhere. Shadows are hard
(`0 3px 0 rgba(0,0,0,.9)`), never blurred. No gradients as decoration.

**Gold is an accent only** — rules, and the single landing word of a hook. Never
body text, never a whole line.

**The full URL is always visible text.** Never "link in bio" alone, on any
platform. The one link is `https://hyperfollow.com/PhilosophicalKing`.

**Never open on black.** Measured on real retention data: a video that faded in
from black held 48% where the same-length alternatives held 58–75%.

## Imagery

Stylised cinematic illustration in the vintage-35mm-film-still manner — flat
graphic shapes, minimal detail, heavy atmospheric haze and light bloom, soft
vignette, subtle grain. Centred, symmetrical, still.

One recurring character across everything, **the Wanderer**: a solitary figure
seen strictly from behind, face never visible, dead-centre, in a long dark
ground-length hooded robe with the hood up, shoulders squared — a near-black
silhouette with faint teal rim-light. He is never shown from the front. His
identity lives in shape, so it survives even as a cut-out against blown-out
light. Full spec in `../music-assets/movie-shots/CHARACTER.md`.

## Voice

Calm, aphoristic, stoic-modern. Short declarative lines. One idea carries a
piece. Never salesy, no exclamation marks. The tone of a line cut into stone
rather than written for engagement.

## Avoid

Gradients as decoration, rounded corners, blurred shadows, stock-photo warmth,
emoji beyond ♛, any second accent colour, anything resembling a SaaS landing
page. The reference is a title card from a serious film.
