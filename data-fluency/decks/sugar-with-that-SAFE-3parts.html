Part 3 · Policy · Limits and sources

Pilot targeted rules before any ban

Decision we ask for today: approve a six-month Small-by-default pilot with two or three chains.

Start where the data points: the drink cup and the salt on the plate. Measure what people actually order, then decide.

NEXT STEPS

Pilot Small-by-default with two or three chains for six months.

Ask chains for sales data. A menu shows what is offered, not what is eaten.

Repeat with local menus. Portions and recipes differ by country.

WHAT THIS DATA CAN'T TELL US

One chain, one menu. 260 McDonald's items, US nutrition facts.

No sales volumes. A rarely ordered shake counts as much as fries.

Thresholds are ours. 50g sugar and 1,150mg sodium follow WHO and US daily values.
