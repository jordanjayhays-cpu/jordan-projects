## The trap in Session 7

The syllabus says so itself, in bold:

> *"Please note that there are two readings with the same title, but they are different articles."*

Session 7 lists **"Why Spy? - The Psychology of espionage"** and **"The Psychology of espionage"**.
These are **two different articles**. Both are required. A third piece, Harvey Weinstein's Army of
Spies, sits in the same block and is **optional**.

If a reading for this course has ever been wrong, this is the most likely place it went wrong.

### The two that carry real weight

**Session 9, the San Serriffe A and B cases.** The syllabus: *"Working the A and B case to get an
answer is required!"* This session sets up the **group assignment, which is 40 percent of the
grade**. Session 8 has no readings specifically so this work can start early: *"You might start the
work for the next session, which involves a fair amount of reading and work."*

**Session 11, the Chinese REE case.** The syllabus: *"The case for this session is extremely
important; you will be bored stiff if you do not read the case and prepare your answers with
care."* Come with these three answered:

1. Why is there an apparent stoppage in Chinese REE exports?
2. Who or what is responsible for the apparent stoppage?
3. How might you explain their actions?

## How the class runs

Slides are distributed **after** each class, not before. Readings deliberately do not repeat the
lectures: *"they are there to round out your thinking."* Some readings are old on purpose, and the
syllabus is blunt about it: *"If you believe that 'newness' of cases and ideas equates to
importance, please avoid this course."*

Note for taking notes, from the syllabus: *"If you take notes, end every sentence with a question
mark."*

## AI policy

GenAI is **encouraged**, with the same acknowledgement template Ríos uses:

> I acknowledge the use of [AI systems link] to [specify how you used generative AI]. The prompts
> used include [list of prompts]. The output of these prompts was used to [explain how you used the
> outputs in your work].

If no AI was used: *"No content generated by AI technologies has been used in this assignment."*

Same warning as Regional & Urban: *"Don't take ChatGPT's or any GenAI's output at face value. Assume
it is wrong unless you either know the answer or can cross-check it with another source. You are
responsible for any errors or omissions."*
