## Suggested structure (main text, about 950 words)

1. Opening paragraph: role, client, constraints, and the operating principle (human access over technical access, detection risk over take). About 80 words.
2. Six numbered sections, one per question, 120 to 160 words each, every one pointing to an appendix.
3. Closing paragraph: what this teaches about defending IE. The guidelines frame the exercise as learning how to protect an organisation, so one paragraph of defender's lessons closes the loop. About 80 words.

## Appendices to build (bullets and tables allowed here)

- A. Target matrix: department or role, secrets held, access score, vulnerability score, detection risk, priority rank.
- B. Team tasking: Pedro, Mike, Sophie, each with cover, targets, pitch, risk.
- C. Six-month timeline: month by month, from spotting to exit.
- D. Budget: 25,000 euro allocated across covers, gifts, expenses, petty payments, contingency.
- E. Risk and detection register: method, take, discovery risk, mitigation.
- F. Sources: HUMINT session readings, any public reporting on corporate espionage cited in the text.
