## 1. Formation

1.1 Kinsol LLC ("the Company") was organised as a limited liability company under the Colorado
Limited Liability Company Act, C.R.S. § 7-80-101 et seq., by Articles of Organization filed with the
Colorado Secretary of State.

1.2 This Agreement governs the Company's internal affairs and the Member's rights and obligations.

1.3 **Principal place of business:** [YOUR MADRID RESIDENTIAL ADDRESS]

1.4 **Registered agent and registered office in Colorado:** Northwest Registered Agent Service, Inc.,
at its Denver, Colorado address of record.

## 2. Member

2.1 **Sole Member:** Jordan Hays, holding one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interest.

2.2 The Company is **member-managed**. The Member has full and exclusive authority to manage and
control the Company's business, to bind the Company, and to execute any contract, instrument or
agreement on its behalf.

2.3 The Member may open, maintain and close bank, payment-processor and other financial accounts in
the Company's name, and is an authorised signer on each.

## 3. Purpose

3.1 The Company is formed to engage in any lawful business permitted under Colorado law, and in
particular: **business development and consulting services, including referral and introduction
services to recruitment and staffing firms, B2B sales consulting, and market-entry advisory.**

3.2 Where the Company contracts directly with a client, it may engage subcontractors to deliver all
or part of the services and pays them as subcontractors in its own name. Revenue under such a contract
is the Company's revenue.

## 4. Capital

4.1 **Initial capital contribution:** $[AMOUNT — any figure, e.g. 1,000] in cash, contributed by the
Member from personal funds.

4.2 The Member is not obliged to make further contributions, and may make additional contributions at
any time, recorded in the Company's books.

4.3 No interest accrues on capital contributions.

## 5. Distributions, profits and losses

5.1 All profits and losses are allocated entirely to the Member.

5.2 Distributions are made at the times and in the amounts the Member determines, provided the Company
remains able to pay its debts as they fall due.

5.3 The Member will keep Company funds in Company accounts, separate from personal funds, and will
record distributions and reimbursements in the Company's books. [This clause exists to protect your
limited liability — honour it in practice, not just on paper.]

## 6. Tax treatment

6.1 The Company is a single-member LLC and is by default a **disregarded entity** for US federal
income tax purposes under Treas. Reg. § 301.7701-3, reported on the Member's return.

6.2 The Company holds an Employer Identification Number issued by the IRS. [EIN letter received

## 7. Books and records

7.1 The Company maintains complete and accurate books at its principal place of business or with its
bookkeeper, including a record of contributions, distributions, and separate ledgers for each line of
business the Company bills for.

7.2 The Company's fiscal year ends **31 December**.

## 8. Liability and indemnification

8.1 The Member is not personally liable for the Company's debts, obligations or liabilities solely by
reason of being a member, except as required by Colorado law.

8.2 The Company indemnifies the Member against claims, costs and losses arising from acts performed in
good faith on the Company's behalf, other than those arising from fraud, wilful misconduct, or a
knowing violation of law.

## 9. Transfers and admission of members

9.1 The Member may transfer all or part of the membership interest, and may admit additional members,
by written amendment to this Agreement.

9.2 On admission of any additional member, this Agreement is to be replaced by a multi-member
operating agreement.

## 10. Dissolution

10.1 The Company dissolves on the Member's written decision to dissolve, or as required by law.

10.2 On dissolution, assets are applied first to creditors, then to the Member.

## 11. General

11.1 **Governing law:** Colorado, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.

11.2 **Amendment:** only by a written instrument signed by the Member.

11.3 **Entire agreement:** this Agreement is the complete operating agreement of the Company and
supersedes any prior understanding.

11.4 If any provision is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in effect.
