## How it works

1. AmigoSales recruits reps on LinkedIn: Spanish-speaking university students in Madrid (Complutense, UAM, Carlos III, IE, Comillas; business, marketing, tourism).
2. Each rep gets a route: 10-15 studios in one barrio (address, Google rating, hours).
3. The rep visits, pitches in Spanish, and signs the studio up on the spot through the partner page (book.massageclub.io/partner).
4. A sign-up counts only when the studio's menu is live on book.massageclub.io.

Target: 3-5 reps, about 2 barrios each. Suggested first barrios: Chamberí, Malasaña, Salamanca.

## Field kit (to build when this goes live)

- Visit windows: 11:00-13:00 and 16:00-18:00, outside the busiest hours.

## Rep pay (recommended structure, amounts not set)

Massage Club is paid per massage by the studio. The rep earns a share of Massage Club's fee, not of the studio's price.

| | Structure | Tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Safe (recommended) | €[__] per live studio + [__]% of Massage Club's fee on that studio's bookings for 12 months | Pays from day one and rewards studios that actually get booked; ends after a year |
| Bold | Royalty only, larger share, 24 months | Nothing up front for Massage Club, but students may not wait months to get paid |
| Weird | Rep "owns" a barrio: royalty on every studio in it | Reps act like owners; hard to track fairly |

Illustrative math (placeholders only, do not quote to reps): €50 average massage × 10% Massage Club fee [UNSURE] = €5; rep gets 20% = €1 per massage. At 20 bookings a month, a studio earns its rep about €20 a month, so 10 busy studios come to about €200 a month.

Rules for the rep agreement:
- A studio counts as live when its menu is published and it has taken its first booking.
- The royalty runs 12 months from the studio's first booking, is paid monthly, and covers completed bookings only.
- One rep per studio: whoever logs the first visit owns it.
- The royalty stops if the studio leaves Massage Club.

## Premortem

1. Fake or empty sign-ups. Fix: pay only on a verified live menu, plus a first-booking bonus or royalty.
2. Reps quit after a few rejections. Fix: Jordan or Dani walks in to 5 studios first; the objection answers that actually work go into the script.
3. Reps over-promise (prices, "free forever"). Fix: one approved script and FAQ; nothing promised beyond it.
