Investor Overview · Thesis Stage · 2026

Remote work is repricing Southeast Asia's islands, and the pattern is always the same: the community arrives first, the capital arrives late, and whoever stood between them owns the upside. Bali already ran this play. The Philippines — English-speaking, at half the cost — hasn't yet.

PHREGROUP is built to stand in that gap: the demand engine first, property only behind proven occupancy.

The Model

I

Audience

Honest island documentaries on YouTube — internet speed tests, real prices, real footage. Content builds the list.

II

Community

A curated waitlist of remote workers committing to month-plus stays. Demand becomes measurable before a peso is spent.

III

Partnership

Waitlist in hand, we contract with local property owners — guaranteed occupancy for preferred rates. Asset-light.

IV

Property

Only where occupancy is proven do we acquire and develop long-stay assets — with local partners, on local knowledge.

Why the Philippines

Language

The only large English-speaking island nation in the region — the friction that keeps long-stay westerners out of most of Asia doesn't exist here.

Cost basis

Our scouted first location runs $500–800/month all-in with workable fiber — a cost base Bali and Thailand lost years ago.

Ground network

Founder network in the Philippine staffing industry — local relationships precede the first acquisition, not the other way around.

Timing

7,000+ islands, no defined nomad capital yet. The winner will be chosen by community, not geography — whoever builds the community picks the island.
