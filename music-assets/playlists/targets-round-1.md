# Playlist outreach, round one

Fourteen real Spotify playlists whose curators publish a submission address in
the playlist description. Found by searching the web index rather than the
Spotify API, so no credentials were needed. Every address below is quoted as
the curator published it.

**Check each playlist before sending.** These came out of a search index, so a
description may have changed since it was crawled. Open the link, read the
description, confirm the address is still there and the playlist is still being
updated. Two minutes per pitch.

Fit is my judgement of whether philosophy rap belongs there, not a measure of
the playlist's quality.

## Do not pitch without checking first

**NEXT UP — Emerging Artists**, Teck Media, teckmedia2021@gmail.com,
teckmediallc.com. The description says *"submit your latest release for curated
playlist placement"*. An LLC offering "placement" is the shape of a paid
service, and paying for placement is the one thing that can get the whole
DistroKid catalogue pulled. If any reply mentions a fee, stop and record it
here so nobody pitches them again.

## Track to pitch

Lead with the track, not the catalogue. Suggested openers by room:

- money and value → **Cash Rules Nothing Around Me**
- a thinking-person's list → **Minds Divided** or **The Fly in the Glass**
- mortality, weight, gravity → **First Step, Last Breathe** or **Denial of Death**
- calmer, lo-fi leaning lists → **Cult of One** or **Uncarved Block**
