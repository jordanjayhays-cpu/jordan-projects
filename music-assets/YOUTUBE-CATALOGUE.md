# The full songs are on YouTube — on a second channel

Corrects the standing note in `PK-claude-project.md` that said full tracks are
not retrievable from YouTube. They are. There are **two** channels and the
catalogue lives on the one we were not looking at.

| | Channel ID | What is on it |
|---|---|---|
| `@PhilosophicalKingMusic` | `UCDmgAVKpqgiyL4QbwQ5aP9Q` | 12 Shorts — the pipeline's own lyric teasers, plus one hand-made *The Cave (Official Video)* |
| **`Philosophical King - Topic`** | **`UCST1Tzuraa0CSR4o3RkVIMw`** | **257 full songs**, auto-generated by DistroKid |

`music-assets/youtube-tracks.json` holds all 257: title, videoId, watch URL, and
the pipeline slug where one matches.

## Why this was missed

The Topic channel is **search-suppressed**. Searching YouTube or YouTube Music
for "Philosophical King", "Philosophical King - Topic", or any individual track
title returns nothing — not the channel, not one song. Our own notes had already
recorded that suppression, and it was still read as "the songs are not there."

Absence from search proves nothing here. Direct lookup by ID works fine.

## Reading the catalogue from here

Bulk enumeration is blocked for an unauthenticated client — the uploads playlist
reports "257 videos" and then hides all of them. What works:

- **`/youtubei/v1/next`** with `playlistId=UUST1Tzuraa0CSR4o3RkVIMw` (the `UC`→`UU`
  uploads-playlist trick) and `playlistIndex` stepping by 40. Returns ~79–99 panel
  entries per call; six calls covered all 257.
- **oEmbed** (`/oembed?url=...`) for a single video — title and channel, always works.
- **RSS** (`/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=...`) — the 15 most recent only.

What does *not* work: the `browse` endpoint on the playlist (hides everything),
the `player` endpoint (returns `ERROR` for these Art Tracks), YouTube search,
YouTube Music search, and scraping the channel page (bot-gated, captcha).

## Durations

30 tracks have confirmed lengths, from a prior session, ranging **128 to 328
seconds**. Against the 30-second iTunes preview the pipeline currently renders
from, that is the whole point: these are full songs.

The other 227 durations are unknown from here — the `player` endpoint is gated
for Art Tracks.

## What this does and does not unlock

It gives every track a **stable, linkable full-song URL**. That is immediately
useful for Substack embeds and Reddit posts.

It does **not** automatically solve audio sourcing. The renderer needs a WAV, and
these are streams, not files. Pulling audio down from YouTube is a separate
question with its own terms-of-service and reliability problems, and it should
not be assumed just because the URLs now exist. Jordan's own source files remain
the clean path to full-length audio.

Note also that the caption link rule is unchanged: the only link in a social
caption is `https://hyperfollow.com/PhilosophicalKing`. These URLs are for
Substack, Reddit, and internal reference.
