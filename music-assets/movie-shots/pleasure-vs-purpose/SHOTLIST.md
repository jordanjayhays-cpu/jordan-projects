# Pleasure vs Purpose — mini movie shot list

30s, 11 shots at 2.73s. Same Wanderer (`../CHARACTER.md`).

**Persisting object: the seed.** It is in his hand or in frame in every single shot —
glowing at the start, held loosely while he's dazzled, still glowing when everything else
has burnt out, then buried, then grown. That through-line is what makes this a story
rather than eleven illustrations of a debate.

**Story:** he's given a seed → offered two paths → takes the bright one → is dazzled →
the sparks burn out → he's left on scorched ground still holding it → turns back → kneels
on hard earth → digs → roots take → a tree stands where he knelt.

The lyrics argue hedonism against eudaimonia and land on Epicurus: balance, "planting the
seed." The film doesn't argue — it shows one man doing both and finding out.
