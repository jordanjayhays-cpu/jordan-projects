# Family businesses: outreach channel research (global)

Goal: find where AmigoSales can reach family businesses and family offices, and what problem to lead with.
Material used: live sources checked 2026-10-30 (linked below) + IE report "La evolución del family office en los mercados privados" (2026).
Done when: Jordan picks 1 region + 1 target + the first 2 channels to test.

## Conclusion

1. **Lead with growth, not governance.** Family businesses' double-digit sales growth fell from 43% (2023) to 25% (2025) (PwC, 1,325 respondents, 62 countries). 65% name technology and 64% digital transformation as top growth priorities. That is the opening for an outbound sales engine.
2. **Advisors are the scalable channel.** FFI is the main global association of family-business advisors; they handle governance and succession, not pipeline. Refer-and-share partnerships beat cold outreach to owners.
3. **Associations give access; they do not sell it.** Enter through a sponsor, an advisor or IE, never as a cold vendor.

Messaging implication: "growth slowed, you want tech-led growth, and you will not trade the family's reputation for it." Outbound must be careful, named and on-brand (78% / 77% are protecting legacy).

## Recommended first moves

1. **Advisor pilot:** shortlist 10 FFI-member advisors in one region and offer a referral share: "you advise on succession and governance, we build the pipeline."
2. **IE door:** email the IE Center as a student offering help with the Family Offices Knowledge Hub; ask for a guest slot in Family Business 4.0.
3. **One association:** one event in one country (ADEFAM or FBUK), 5 owner conversations.
4. **Measure (30 days):** 2 advisor partners + 1 paid pilot. Less = wrong offer or channel.

## Premortem

1. Associations reject vendors → go in via a sponsor, an advisor or IE.
2. Advisors see a competitor → position as execution they do not do, with a referral share.
3. Offer splits between family offices and operating businesses → pick one for 30 days (recommended: operating businesses, AmigoSales' core).
