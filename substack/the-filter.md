There's one line in this song I keep coming back to.

*Imagination replaced with society's face.*

That's the whole thing, really. Not that the world got worse. That something got swapped out quietly enough that you never noticed the trade.

When you're a kid you look at a cloud and see a castle. Nobody taught you that. You just had an unfiltered thing pointed at the sky. Then somewhere along the way you learn what clouds are supposed to look like, and what's worth posting, and what people will think. The castle stops showing up.

*We used to see castles and cardboard in clouds.*
*Now we scroll to a numb, lost deep in the crowd.*

The song calls the thing that did it practicality. It killed what was wild and bold. We traded the fire for silver and gold, and on paper it isn't even a bad trade. That's what makes it hard to see. Nobody hands you a form to sign.

Then the last line turns it around:

*But thoughts are just habits.*

Which is smaller and better news than it sounds. A filter you were born with would be a wall. A habit is only a habit. It can go.
