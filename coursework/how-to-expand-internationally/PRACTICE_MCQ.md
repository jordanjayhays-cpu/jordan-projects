### Plamar and factoring

1. In June 2003 Plamar's estimated cost for a plant abroad producing 300,000 handles and 300,000 seals a month was:
   a) USD 100,000 b) USD 300,000 c) USD 1 million d) USD 3 million

2. On Plamar's 31 Dec 2002 balance sheet, accounts receivable were roughly what share of total assets?
   a) One tenth b) One fifth c) One third d) One half

3. Converted at the rate used in class, Plamar's cash in hand was approximately:
   a) USD 7,000 b) USD 70,000 c) USD 700,000 d) USD 97 million

4. The decisive reason given in class for choosing Guatemala was:
   a) Largest plastics market in the region b) Free trade agreement with Mexico giving access to the US c) Shared currency with Venezuela d) Lowest corporate tax in Latin America

5. Brazil was dismissed as Plamar's destination mainly because:
   a) It had no plastics industry b) Lula had just taken office and the market was too big and expensive for Plamar's funds c) Portuguese language d) It banned foreign plastics firms

6. Factoring without recourse means:
   a) The seller must buy back unpaid invoices b) The factor absorbs the debtor's default risk and receivables leave the balance sheet c) Only one invoice is financed at a time d) The bank charges no fee

7. The main difference between discounting and factoring is:
   a) Discounting is cheaper b) Factoring covers the whole receivables book; discounting is bill by bill and always with recourse c) Factoring requires a letter of credit d) Discounting is only for exports

8. Factoring works best for an SME whose:
   a) Debtors are high quality b) Debtors are small and fragmented c) Sales are in cash d) Receivables are overdue

9. Plamar's typical sources of finance before 2003 were:
   a) Bank loans and bonds b) Venture capital c) Suppliers and own cash flow d) Government grants

10. Why did banks refuse Plamar loans?
    a) Lack of guarantees or collateral b) The company was loss-making c) It had no export sales d) Venezuelan law forbade SME lending

11. About what share of Plamar's annual sales fell between September and December, and why?
    a) 20 percent, school season b) 50 percent, Venezuelans paint homes before Christmas c) 75 percent, harvest d) 10 percent, holidays

12. Plamar's first export customer was in:
    a) Guatemala b) Bolivia c) The Dominican Republic d) Brazil

13. Typical factoring cost mentioned in class:
    a) 0.1 percent b) 1 to 2 percent of receivables c) 10 percent d) 25 percent
