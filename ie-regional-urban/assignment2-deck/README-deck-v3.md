## Rubric → where the points are earned

- **Quality of diagnosis.** Slide 7 runs one test across four candidate constraints for two countries and gets different verdicts. Slides 8–11 show the mechanism behind each verdict.
- **Use of evidence.** Every slide carries its sources in the footer strip. A1 lists every indicator with year and series code.
- **Policy recommendations.** Each programme names a pilot, an IDB instrument, a measure and a precedent. Slide 17 sequences them; slide 18 commits to three published counts.
- **Presentation quality.** One idea per slide, one highlight colour per country, direct labels, no legends.
- **Teamwork and Q&A.** A4 lists the counterarguments; A2 the loan sizes. Split: slides 1–6, 7–11, 12–13, 14–17, 18–19 gives five speakers roughly four minutes each.
