## Shortlist — 5 gap brands realistic for a small sourcing agent

Ranked by (a) verified absence, (b) whether the company actually appoints small/exclusive country distributors.

1. **Dr. Bronner's** — the only VERIFIED gap; formal online application for exclusive international distribution partners (MOQ + marketing commitment required). Natural fit for Good Price/premium UB retail. Inquiry: https://info.drbronner.com/wholesale-customers-u-s/wholesale-customers-international/
2. **Church & Dwight / Arm & Hammer** — Global Markets Group exists precisely to serve small markets via distributors; one deal brings Arm & Hammer + OxiClean + Waterpik. Inquiry: https://churchdwight.com/international.aspx
3. **Burt's Bees** — gap + Clorox runs it with regional distributor partners; small-market friendly relative to core Clorox. Policy/contact: https://www.thecloroxcompany.com/retailer-policy/burts-bees/
4. **Clorox (core cleaning line)** — International segment sells via authorized distributors in Latin America/Asia-Pacific; larger org, slower, but the mechanism exists. Route via Clorox International contact.
5. **Duracell or Energizer** (conditional — presence UNSURE, verify in-store first) — both run distributor programs; Energizer explicitly courts distributors. Only pitch after an on-the-ground shelf check in UB.

Deprioritized and why: **P&G brands** (has its own Mongolia channel already — Gillette/Pampers — and does not appoint small agents), **SC Johnson/Method** (privately held giant, no open distributor program found), **Reckitt/Lysol** (giant; Asia strategy is Dettol), **Georgia-Pacific** (Koch-owned, domestic consumer focus, paper freight economics to landlocked Mongolia are poor), **Seventh Generation** (Unilever-owned).

## Caveats (honesty section)

- E-Mart Mongolia (Sky Hypermarket LLC) skews Korean brands; Good Price Market (Shangri-La mall, UB) already imports American groceries — both are secondary buyer targets besides Nomin.
