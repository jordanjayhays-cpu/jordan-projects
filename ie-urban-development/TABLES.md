## THE FINDING THAT CHANGES THE ARGUMENT

**Mactan Economic Zone is shedding jobs.** Since 2023, **7,883 workers** have lost jobs at MEPZ,
including a single event of about **4,000** and a further **337** reported more recently. In April
2026 PEZA announced it was targeting **1,000 new jobs to offset displacements** — an admission that
the zone is contracting.

Set that beside the Pax Silica decision of the same month and the thesis sharpens from a
counterfactual into an observable trend:

> **Cebu's incumbent electronics-export economy was contracting in the same month the Philippines
> sited its first AI-native industrial hub 600 km north in Luzon.**

That is a far stronger sentence than anything about what Cebu "could not have hosted," and it is
evidenced rather than inferred. **Lead section 2 with it.**

### Note A — the population conflict
Two projections for 2030 circulate: ~3.3M and JICA's 3.8M. They are not reconcilable and the gap is
half a million people. **Pick JICA's** — it is the source your report is built on, it is the figure
planners use, and citing your own primary source consistently is worth more than splitting the
difference. Say in a footnote that other estimates are lower.

### Note B — do not put both poverty figures in the same column
**31–32.5% is a first-semester estimate. 16.9% is a full-year estimate.** They use different
reference periods and are not comparable; a marker who knows PSA methodology will spot it instantly.
**Use the full-year 2023 figure (16.9%) for the province** and the national full-year figure beside
it, or use semestral for both. Never one of each. This is the single most likely way to lose marks
on data handling in this report.

### Note C — this is the pivot of the whole argument, and it is now verified
**The MCDCB has no legal or institutional powers, unlike the MMDA.** It is a coordinating and
planning consortium of the Province plus 13 LGUs — Carcar, Naga, Talisay, Cebu, Mandaue, Lapu-Lapu,
Danao, San Fernando, Minglanilla, Cordova, Consolacion, Liloan, Compostela — together with regional
line agencies and civil society. It formulates plans and coordinates; it cannot enforce. There is an
active legislative push to convert it into a Metro Cebu Development Authority with real powers.

**Why this carries the report:** water and trunk transport are metro-scale systems being decided at
municipal scale by thirteen separately elected governments. That is not a coordination difficulty,
it is a structural impossibility. Every other constraint in your section 3 descends from it.

## CORRECTION to the table above

I earlier recorded the 90% wastewater treatment figure as the 2024–27 Sewerage Master Plan's target.
**That was wrong.** The JICA roadmap states the targets plainly:

> *"it was targeted that the waste water treatment system cover **50% of the population in 2030 and
> 90% in 2050**."*

**90% is a 2050 target, not a near-term one.** And the roadmap's framing of the problem is blunter
than any paraphrase: *"waste water has been hardly treated to begin with. Consequently, the water
quality of the metropolis is worsening."* Quote that line directly — it does the work of a paragraph.
