# AI Medical Transcription Research — Cost Savings Opportunity for Craig Hospital (Denver)

*Research date: August 15, 2026*

## 1. The problem being researched

Craig Hospital (Englewood/Denver, CO) is a 93-bed, private, not-for-profit long-term acute
care and rehabilitation hospital specializing in spinal cord injury (SCI) and traumatic
brain injury (TBI) rehabilitation. Like most hospitals, it produces physician transcription
reports as part of its clinical documentation (its reports are shared through Colorado's
health information exchange).

**Stated benchmark: the hospital pays ~$0.18 (18 cents) per word for transcription.**

Two important notes on that benchmark:

- The medical transcription industry usually prices per **line** (65 characters, roughly
  10–12 words), not per word. Market rates in 2026 run **$0.09–$0.18 per line** and
  $1.10–$4.50 per dictated minute. If Craig is genuinely paying 18¢ per **word**, that is
  roughly 10x above the market rate for human transcription — meaning there are savings
  available even before AI enters the picture.
- Either way, AI transcription/ambient documentation replaces per-unit pricing with a flat
  per-clinician subscription, which is where the large structural savings come from.

## 2. What the cost math looks like

### At the stated 18¢/word rate

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Average clinical note length | ~350 words |
| Cost per note at $0.18/word | ~$63 |
| Notes per clinician per day | 12 |
| Working days per month | 20 |
| **Monthly transcription cost per clinician** | **~$15,120** |

### AI alternative (flat subscription)

| Vendor tier | Typical 2026 price per clinician/month |
|---|---|
| Budget AI scribes (Freed, DeepCura, etc.) | $99–$299 |
| Ambience Healthcare (AutoScribe core) | ~$233–$267 |
| Microsoft Dragon Copilot / DAX | $150–$600 (Flex tier ~$605 for small practices; ~$700 one-time implementation per provider) |
| Abridge | Enterprise contract (comparable enterprise range) |

Even at the top of the enterprise range (~$600/mo), AI documentation is **~96% cheaper**
than the per-word model above. Industry analyses report a **25x–78x cost difference**
between AI and human transcription: at 400 encounters/month, an AI service can cost ~$129
total versus **$3,200–$10,000** for human transcription, and a practice with 20
encounters/day saves an estimated **$36,000–$118,000 per year** by switching.

Human transcription per-encounter cost runs **$8–$25**; AI-assisted hybrid services go as
low as $0.09/line.

## 4. Evidence of outcomes at peer institutions

- A study of **8,581 ambulatory clinicians** across Mass General Brigham, Emory
  Healthcare, UCSF, Yale New Haven Health, and UC Davis (using Ambience, Nuance DAX
  Copilot, and Abridge, all Epic-integrated) found ambient AI scribes reduced total EHR
  time by **13.4 minutes** and documentation time by **16.0 minutes**, with **0.49 more
  visits per week** per clinician.
- A randomized UW School of Medicine study showed ambient AI notetaking cut record-keeping
  time by **30 minutes daily per provider**.
- **MultiCare Health System** hit **92% clinician adoption**; clinician burnout in ambient
  AI cohorts dropped from **52% to 39%**.
- As of early 2026, **70% of UCSF physicians** were using AI scribes.

## 5. Takeaways for Craig Hospital

1. **Verify the 18¢/word figure and its unit.** If it truly is per word (not per line),
   Craig is paying roughly 10x market rate; renegotiating or re-bidding the human
   transcription contract alone would produce immediate savings.
2. **Structural savings come from moving off per-unit pricing.** A flat AI subscription
   ($100–$600/clinician/month) versus per-word billing yields savings on the order of
   90%+ at typical documentation volumes, with published annual savings of
   $36K–$118K for even a 20-encounter/day practice.
3. **For physician documentation**, Abridge, Dragon Copilot (DAX), and Ambience are the
   proven enterprise choices, with Epic integration and deployments at Kaiser, Mayo,
   Hopkins, Duke, UPMC, Cleveland Clinic, and UCSF.
4. **For Craig's rehab therapy documentation** (PT/OT/SLP — a large share of a SCI/TBI
   hospital's notes), rehab-specific tools like Net Health ReDoc's ambient AI are worth
   piloting, with reported savings of 20–30 minutes per evaluation.
5. **Compliance note:** any vendor must sign a BAA and be HIPAA-compliant; all the
   enterprise vendors above operate under BAAs with health systems.
