# The click list

Open a link. Tap the comment bubble. Follow the people commenting. That is the
whole thing. No searching, no hashtag pages, no deciding who counts.

Checked 2026-08-29. The four posts return 200 from a plain fetch. The account
pages return 429, which is Instagram throttling this server rather than the
accounts being wrong; their titles came from Instagram's own indexed pages.

## Why the commenters, not the account

A page with 40k followers will not follow back. Someone who left a real comment
on it has already proven they care about the subject **and** that they engage
rather than scroll. That is the entire strategy, and it is why this list points
at posts rather than at big accounts.

## The five-second check

Open the profile before following. Three green flags:

1. They have posts of their own, any number above zero
2. Following-to-follower ratio under about 3:1 — 7,000 following against 200
   followers is a farmer
3. Their comments elsewhere vary — the same emoji on fifty accounts is a bot

All three pass, follow. Unsure, skip. There is an endless supply and a bad
follow costs ratio.

Never follow: "follow for follow" bios, giveaway comment sections, huge
accounts, empty accounts.

## How many

**20 to 25 a day, spread across the day.** Never in one burst — Instagram flags
rapid sequential follows and temporarily blocks the action.

Expect 15–25% to follow back from a list this targeted, so 4–6 real followers a
day. The same 25 chosen at random returns about 5%, and those never listen to
anything.

Unfollow non-reciprocators after about ten days so the ratio stays healthy.

## Before any of this: the free part

**Follow back everyone who has already touched PK.** Highest follow-back rate of
anything here and it costs nothing. Five people subscribed on YouTube on Aug 25
and none have been acknowledged.

## The one that beats all of it

The people who used Bayanihan's audio. They chose the music; nobody on this page
has. That list only exists inside the apps:

**Instagram** → Search → `Bayanihan Philosophical King` → **Audio** tab → tap the
track → every Reel that used it.
**TikTok** → same query → **Sounds** tab.
**Facebook** → under Reels, and this is where the 212k actually happened.

No tool can surface these; audio pages need a logged-in session. Ten minutes in
the app beats everything above.

## Never automate this

Instagram bans API-driven following and would restrict the account. No tool, no
script, no service.
