# PK follower growth — daily follow targets

## The change from "follow this account" to "mine this post"

Jordan asked for one account a day to follow. Following the big niche accounts
themselves is close to worthless — a 40k-follower Stoicism page will not follow back a
0-follower artist. **Follow-back lives in the comments.** People who take the time to
comment on a Kapwa post are engaged, usually have small accounts themselves, and follow
back at a far higher rate than the page they're commenting on.

So the daily delivery is **a target post, not a target account**: a live, recent post in
PK's exact niche, with an active comment section. Jordan opens it and follows 15–25
commenters. That takes the same five minutes and converts several times better.

This also fits what each side can actually do. Instagram and TikTok are login-gated — I
can find high-signal *posts* through web search, but I cannot see follower counts,
comment sections, or whether an account is active. Jordan can see all of that in two
taps. So: I find the vein, he mines it.

**Automated following is off the table.** Instagram bans API-driven follows and will
restrict or kill the account. Every follow is manual, by Jordan, from his phone. This is
deliberate, not a limitation to engineer around.

## Daily quota

- **Instagram:** 20–30 follows/day, spread across the day, never in one burst.
  Under ~50/day stays well clear of Instagram's action blocks on a young account.
- **TikTok:** 10–15/day. Lower priority — see below.
- Unfollow non-reciprocators after ~10 days to keep the ratio from looking desperate.

## Priority order — why Instagram first

TikTok's For You page serves zero-follower accounts, so follower count barely gates reach
there. Instagram's does not: a 0-follower account posting daily reads as a bot and gets
suppressed. So **Instagram is where following actually buys something**, and TikTok is
where *content quality* buys reach instead. Put the manual effort into Instagram.

## What makes a good target post

1. **Filipino-values content first** — Kapwa, Bayanihan, pagkatao, Filipino identity.
   This is PK's proven audience: Bayanihan did 212k plays, ~95% Philippines. These
   commenters are the closest thing to a warm list that exists.
2. **Stoicism / memento mori / philosophy-for-normal-people** — second tier, global,
   converts less but matches the catalogue.
3. **Posted within the last ~7 days**, so commenters are still active.
4. **Comment count in the tens or low hundreds** — enough to mine, not so many that it's
   a giveaway thread full of bots.

Avoid: giveaway posts, engagement-pod threads, huge celebrity accounts, anything where
comments are mostly emoji.

## Honest expectations

At 25 follows/day with a 15–25% follow-back rate on well-chosen commenters, that's
roughly **4–6 new followers a day, 120–180 in the first month.** It is slow and it is
manual. The point isn't the number — it's getting off zero so the account stops reading
as abandoned, which is what's currently suppressing everything else.

The faster lever is still the content: the Filipino-themed tracks are the proven
performers, and the queue is currently ordered by earnings rather than by reach. Moving
Bayanihan, Kapwa and the other Filipino-values tracks to the front would do more for
growth than any amount of following.
