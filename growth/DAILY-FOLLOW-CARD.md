# The daily follow — do this on your phone, 10 minutes

Replaces the automated target emails, which never worked. Instagram blocks search
engines from indexing post content, so no research tool can hand you a live post
with an active comment section. You can see it; nothing I run can. So the search
happens on your phone, and this card is the procedure.

---

## The one rule

**Never follow the big account. Follow the people commenting under it.**

A 40k Stoicism page will not follow you back. The person who left a real comment
on it might — they have already proven they care about the subject and they
engage rather than scroll. That is the whole strategy.

---

## Where to search, in priority order

**Filipino first.** It is the only audience PK has ever actually had — Bayanihan
did 212,000 plays, 95% of everything, from Filipino Reels using the audio.
Everything else in the catalogue is unproven.

| Priority | Instagram / TikTok search | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `#kapwa` `#bayanihan` `#filipinovalues` `#pinoyquotes` | The proven audience |
| 2 | `#filipinopride` `#pinoy` `#ofw` | Same audience, wider |
| 3 | `#stoicism` `#dailystoic` `#mementomori` | The stated niche |
| 4 | `#philosophy` `#existentialism` | Broad, weakest |
| 5 | `#unsignedartist` `#spokenword` `#lofihiphop` | Peer artists — highest follow-back of any tier |

Rotate one per day so you are not hitting the same crowd repeatedly.

## Instagram — the exact taps

1. Tap the **magnifying glass** (bottom bar)
2. Tap the **search field**, type `#kapwa`, hit search
3. Tap the **Tags** tab, then the hashtag itself
4. Tap **Recent**, not Top — *this matters*. Top posts have thousands of comments
   that are mostly bots and giveaway hunters. Recent posts have twenty real ones.
5. Open a post that has comments
6. Tap the **speech bubble** to open the comment list
7. Tap a **commenter's username** — their profile opens
8. Tap **Follow**
9. Tap **back** twice, next commenter

**Do 20–25, spread across the day.** Not in one burst — Instagram flags rapid
sequential follows and will temporarily block the action.

---

## TikTok — the exact taps

1. Tap the **magnifying glass** (top right)
2. Type `#bayanihan`, search
3. Tap the **Videos** tab
4. Open a video, tap the **comment bubble** on the right
5. Tap a **commenter's profile picture**
6. Tap **Follow**
7. Back, next commenter

**Do 10–15.** Lower priority than Instagram, and the reason is structural: TikTok's
For You page serves zero-follower accounts based on watch-through, so following
buys you much less there. Instagram genuinely gates reach on follower count for
young accounts, which is why the manual grind is worth it there and only there.

---

## The five-second check before each follow

Open the profile. Three green flags:

1. **They have posts of their own** — any number above zero
2. **Following-to-follower ratio under ~3:1** — 7,000 following / 200 followers is a farmer
3. **Their comments elsewhere vary** — the same emoji on fifty accounts is a bot

All three pass, follow. Unsure, skip — there is an endless supply and a bad
follow costs you ratio.

**Never follow:** "follow for follow" bios, giveaway comment sections, huge
accounts, empty accounts, or anything you would not want to appear next to.

---

## Before you start: the free part

**Follow back everyone who has already liked, commented on, or followed PK.**
Highest follow-back rate of anything here, costs nothing, and it is currently
being ignored. Within 48 hours of them touching your content — after a week they
have forgotten you and it is just a follow from a stranger.

---

## What to expect

At 25 a day into a chosen list: **15–25% follow back**, so 4–6 real followers a
day, roughly 120–180 in the first month. The same 25 chosen at random gets you
about 5%, and those never listen to anything.

Unfollow non-reciprocators after ~10 days so the ratio does not look desperate.

**Ignore the follower number for the first month.** The point is getting off zero
so the account stops reading as abandoned — which is what currently suppresses
everything else you do there.

---

## Never automate this

Instagram bans API-driven following and would restrict the account. No tool, no
script, no service. The ten minutes is the cost of the channel.
