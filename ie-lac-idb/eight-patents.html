Take the wall down

International benchmarks · This region

This has already been done. Here. On purpose

Three cases from Latin America, each engineered by a state that did one narrow thing for a long time. None of it was luck. All of it was policy.

Chile

Fundación Chile

Learned to farm salmon itself, proved it worked, then handed the industry to private firms and stepped back.

Costa Rica

CINDE

Went from bananas to medical devices in one generation by targeting a narrow set of investors and keeping them.

Medellín

Social urbanism

Built cable cars into the hillsides the city had written off. It was never a transport project.

Each one a state doing a narrow thing for longer than one electoral term.

Programme one

Fund a capability agency

One agency, one country, one industry at a time. It learns the industry itself, de-risks it, then hands it to firms and withdraws.

Years 0–2Felt inside one electoral term

HOW IT WORKS

  • Pick one tradeable industry with a real window open now
  • State carries the first-mover risk, not the firms
  • Hand over on a published date and withdraw
  • Fund the agency, not the factories

Precedent: Fundación Chile, CINDE Costa Rica

Programme two

Reach the towns outside

Perroux's growth pole has five stages. Two of them fail by default, and those are the two the money attaches to.

Years 5–10Needs the tax base first

HOW IT WORKS

  • Roads and signal reach the towns outside the pole
  • Local firms learn to supply the centre
  • Skills spread outward instead of draining in
  • The last two stages never happen on their own

Case: Medellín's cable cars were stage five, not a transport project

Programme three

Take the wall down

Pay for health and pensions from general tax, not from wages. This costs money before it saves money, and it is the only one that pays back inside one term.

Years 0–2The only one that pays back fast

HOW IT WORKS

  • Stop taxing the act of being visible
  • Firms can pass ten employees without hiding

  • Say the cost out loud before anyone asks

WHAT THIS CANNOT TELL US

Patents measure registered invention, not all ingenuity, and firms often file abroad instead. R&D years differ by country, 2023 to 2024. Korea's path had a geopolitical sponsor and an authoritarian state. One country is not a counterfactual for another.
