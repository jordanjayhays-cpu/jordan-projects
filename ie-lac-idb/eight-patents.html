Policy recommendations · Programme one

Fund a capability agency

One agency, one country, one industry at a time.

Years 0–2Felt inside one electoral term
Key

It learns the industry itself, de-risks it, then hands it to firms and withdraws on a published date.

how it works

  • Pick one tradeable industry with a real window open now
  • State carries the first-mover risk, not the firms
  • Hand over on a published date and withdraw
  • Fund the agency, not the factories

Policy recommendations · Programme two

Reach the towns outside

Perroux’s growth pole has five stages. Two of them fail by default.

Years 5–10Needs the tax base first
Key

The faded stages happen without us. The two the money attaches to are the ones that never happen on their own.

how it works

  • Roads and signal reach the towns outside the pole
  • Local firms learn to supply the centre
  • Skills spread outward instead of draining in
  • The last two stages never happen on their own

What this cannot tell us

Patents measure registered invention, not all ingenuity, and firms often file abroad instead. R&D years differ by country, 2023 to 2024. Korea’s path had a geopolitical sponsor and an authoritarian state. One country is not a counterfactual for another.
