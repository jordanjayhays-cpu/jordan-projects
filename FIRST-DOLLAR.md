# Getting out of the 95% — the first-dollar plan

Written 2026-10-05. The base rate: ~95% of built things never earn (US Census business
applications → employer formations ≈ 5.6%; Indie Hackers products >$100k/yr ≈ 5%).
Jordan is at 0 earning of 61 projects.

## The diagnosis

**Not one of the 61 projects has a price and a named buyer at the same time.**

That is the entire gap. It is not build quality, features, automation, or tooling — on building,
Jordan is an extreme outlier (40 shipped against a platform median of ~1). On earning he is exactly
average. The missing activity is not building. It is naming a price to a named person and waiting
for the answer.

## Correction to earlier advice in this project

**"Send the 11 Massage Club customers" was never a money action.** The MC model is: client books via
MC, pays the studio the full rate, MC reimburses test bookings. So every successful booking costs
money. If all 11 convert, MC is poorer and better informed.

That is a legitimate experiment. It is not revenue, and it was repeatedly framed as the nearest
dollar. It is not.

**Massage Club's real blocker is that nothing charges anybody.** It needs one of:
- a commission from the studio per booking (market norm for marketplace referral is reportedly
  15–25% — **UNVERIFIED for Madrid spas, one conversation settles it**)
- a booking fee from the client
- a subscription / pass

Until one exists, no amount of working automation moves MC out of the 95%.

## Everything else: frozen, not killed

27 built and 13 live projects compete for the only genuinely scarce resource — attention on asking
people for money. Under Reinertsen's WIP logic, high utilisation collapses throughput non-linearly.
Freezing is not giving up; it is what makes the three above actually happen.

Frozen until one of the three earns: Niah (go/no-go 15 Oct stands), Vaya and all sub-brands, Sauce
Match, References & Reputations, Cup Takeover, Nomin, COMARE, Puhunan, New Bali, every built asset,
every idea.

## The three rules

1. **Nothing gets built until it has a price and a named buyer written down.** Not a market, not a
   segment — a name.
2. **Freeze the rest.** One lane at a time.
3. **One ask per day, to a named human, about money.** Not outreach — a price and a question.
