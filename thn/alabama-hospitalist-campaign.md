## Target market (research)

- **Buyers, in order:** (1) hospitalist medical director / chief of hospital medicine, (2) CMO or VP Medical Affairs, (3) CEO at rural and community hospitals.
- **Why now:**
  - SHM 2025: 64% of hospital medicine groups expect to add FTEs; growth, not turnover, drives unfilled positions (via ACP CareerSource summary of SHM).
  - Alabama Hospital Association: 27 Alabama rural hospitals at risk of closing, 19 at immediate risk (reported by ABC33/40).
  - CHG 2025: 80% of facilities expect flat or growing locums use.
- **THN's own claims (use carefully, they are THN's numbers):** ~20% annual hospitalist turnover, 8-12 months to fill, ~$210K lost per vacant month, $1.2M-$2M+ annual savings for many programs.
- **The differentiator to lead with:** full-time traveling hospitalists plus an on-site THN physician leader (continuity, not a rotating locums list).
- **Exclude:** large academic centers with in-house float pools (e.g. UAB) for wave 1. UNSURE, confirm with THN.
